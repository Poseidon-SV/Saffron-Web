Transformable Robotics

Morph-QT

Prototype Validated

Morph-QT is a transformable quadrotor platform that transitions between aerial flight and powered ground mobility. Designed for versatile field operations, it combines compact transformation mechanics, extended ground endurance, obstacle detection, and dual-mode mobility in a single robotic platform.

Key Features

  • Dual-Mode Mobility
    Transitions between aerial flight and powered ground movement for versatile field deployment.
  • Rapid Transformation
    Transforms between mobility modes in approximately 5–10 seconds.
  • Extended Ground Endurance
    Provides up to 3.5 hours of ground operation for prolonged missions.
  • Obstacle Detection
    Integrated sensing provides obstacle detection at ranges of up to 12 metres.
  • Compact Payload Platform
    Supports payloads up to 500 g while maintaining a compact 2.5 kg platform.

Applications

  • Reconnaissance
    Combines aerial overview with ground-level movement for versatile reconnaissance missions.
  • Search & Rescue
    Enables rapid aerial access followed by ground-level inspection in difficult environments.
  • Disaster Response
    Provides flexible mobility for surveying and assessing challenging or inaccessible areas.
  • Inspection & Monitoring
    Supports aerial and terrestrial inspection of infrastructure and complex environments.

Morph-QT brings aerial and ground mobility together in a compact platform, designed to extend robotic access across complex and changing environments.
