What We Offer

Printing Services

End-to-end additive manufacturing tailored to your engineering needs

Rapid Prototyping

Accelerate your development cycle with fast-turnaround functional prototypes. From concept CAD to physical part within 24–48 hours for most geometries.

Functional Parts & Jigs

Production-grade structural components, fixtures, and tooling jigs. Engineered for strength, tolerances, and mechanical fit; not just appearance.

Robotics & Enclosures

Custom mounts, sensor housings, electronics enclosures, and robotic end-effectors designed for real-world deployment and field conditions.

Custom Finishes

Post-processing including sanding, acetone vapor smoothing, painting, and primer coatings for display models and customer-facing parts.

Multi-Material Printing

Combine rigid and flexible materials in a single print run, ideal for gaskets, grippers, and complex assemblies that demand varied mechanical properties.

Design Consultation

Not sure if your design is print-ready? Our engineers review your CAD files for printability, structural integrity, and optimal material selection.

Materials

Material Library

A wide range of engineering-grade filaments and resins for every application

PLA+

General prototyping, visual models, low-stress parts. Easy to post-process.

PETG

Chemical-resistant, semi-flexible, great for enclosures and outdoor use.

ABS / ASA

Heat & UV resistant. Ideal for automotive and field-deployed components.

TPU

Flexible and impact-absorbing. Perfect for grippers, seals, and bumpers.

Nylon (PA12)

High strength, fatigue-resistant. Used in gears, hinges, and load-bearing parts.

Carbon-Filled

Reinforced PETG/Nylon with carbon fiber for maximum stiffness-to-weight ratio.

PEEK / PEI

High-performance polymers for aerospace and extreme-temperature environments.

Resin (SLA)

Ultra-fine detail for complex geometries, dental, and optical components.

How It Works

Our Process

Simple, fast, and transparent from file to finished part

01
Submit Your Files

Upload your STL, STEP, or OBJ files via the quote form below. Include quantity, material preference, and any surface finish requirements.

02
Design Review & Quote

Our engineers review your model for printability and provide a detailed quote within 12–24 hours, including lead time and material recommendations.

03
Print & Quality Check

Parts are printed with calibrated machines and inspected for dimensional accuracy, layer quality, and structural integrity before post-processing.

04
Delivery or Pickup

Finished parts are carefully packaged and shipped across India, or available for pickup at our facility in New Delhi.
