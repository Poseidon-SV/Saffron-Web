Transformable Robotics

Morph T-1700

Completed Prototype

Morph T-1700 is the first functional version of Verham’s multi-mode robotic platform. It established the foundational architecture for hybrid aerial-ground mobility, proving the feasibility of the transformation system and validating real-world control pipelines.

Category
Transformable Robotics
Status
Completed Prototype
Generation
First-Gen Morph Platform

Key Features

  • Core Transformation System
    Original mechanical layout enabling rapid switching between aerial and ground mobility.
  • Integrated Flight & Ground Control
    FC + MC hybrid control pipeline for coordinated aerial and terrestrial operation.
  • Active Sensor Mapping
    Multi-sensor integration for obstacle avoidance and guidance.
  • Modular Frame
    Adaptable chassis supporting further upgrades and Morph platform variants.

Applications

  • R&D Validation
    Core experimental platform for Morph series development.
  • Education & Research
    Demonstrates hybrid locomotion, transformation mechanics, and robotic control.
  • Technology Demonstrations
    Demonstrates the core Morph concept and hybrid mobility capabilities.
  • Prototyping Platform
    Foundation for subsequent Morph platform development and variants.

Morph T-1700 is where the Morph journey began; the platform that proved hybrid mobility can be compact and practical.
