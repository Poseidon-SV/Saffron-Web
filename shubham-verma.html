Founder profile

Shubham Verma

Founder & Director, Verham Robotics

Shubham Verma is the Founder and Director of Verham Robotics, a New Delhi-based robotics company focused on transforming aerial-ground robotic platforms, autonomous systems, UAVs, UGVs and intelligent robotics.

His work spans robotics engineering, embedded electronics, computer vision, autonomous navigation and AI-driven robotic systems, with a focus on developing and testing practical robotic platforms.
