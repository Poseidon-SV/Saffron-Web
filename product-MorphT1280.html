Transformable Robotics

Morph-BT

Under Active Development

Morph-BT is the next evolution in multi-mode autonomous robotics. Designed for demanding missions, it combines ground mobility with aerial capability in a single compact platform. Lightweight transformation mechanics, improved stability, and enhanced embedded control make it the most advanced version in the Morph series.

Key Features

  • Seamless Mode Switching
    Rapid transformation between terrestrial and aerial modes for hybrid deployment.
  • Enhanced Power System
    More efficient energy utilization for longer missions.
  • Improved Structural Rigidity
    Reinforced frame for field durability and payload stability.
  • Advanced Sensor Suite
    Upgraded LiDAR, TOF, and IMU integration for accurate real-world mapping.
  • AI-Driven Control
    Intelligent mode selection, stabilization, and navigation pipeline.

Applications

  • Search & Rescue
    Access hard-to-reach zones through combined flight and ground mobility.
  • Defense & Surveillance
    Stealth movement, low-altitude reconnaissance, and terrain-adaptive deployment.
  • Industrial Inspection
    Hybrid access to rooftops, tight corridors, and complex structures.
  • R&D Platform
    Modular architecture ideal for experimental robotics and AI testing.

Morph-BT represents the future direction of Verham Robotics — compact, intelligent, and mission-ready hybrid systems.
