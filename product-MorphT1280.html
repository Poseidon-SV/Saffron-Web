Morph T-1280

Morph T-1280

  • Category: Transformable Robotics
  • Status: Under Active Development
  • Incubation: IHFC – IIT Delhi

Morph T-1280 is the next evolution in multi-mode autonomous robotics. Designed for demanding missions, it combines ground mobility with aerial capability in a single compact platform. Lightweight transformation mechanics, improved stability, and enhanced embedded control make it the most advanced version in the Morph series.

Key Features

  1. Seamless Mode Switching: Rapid transformation between terrestrial and aerial mode for hybrid deployment.
  2. Enhanced Power System: More efficient energy utilization for longer missions.
  3. Improved Structural Rigidity: Reinforced frame for field durability and payload stability.
  4. Advanced Sensor Suite: Upgraded LiDAR, TOF, and IMU integration for accurate real-world mapping.
  5. AI-Driven Control: Intelligent mode selection, stabilization, and navigation pipeline.

Applications

  • Search & Rescue: Access hard-to-reach zones through combined flight and ground mobility.
  • Defense & Surveillance: Stealth movement, low-altitude recon, and terrain-adaptive deployment.
  • Industrial Inspection: Hybrid access to rooftops, tight corridors, and complex structures.
  • R&D Platform: Modular architecture ideal for experimental robotics and AI testing.
Morph T-1280 represents the future direction of Verham Robotics — compact, intelligent, and mission-ready hybrid systems.
